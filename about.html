About Weight Loss Diet

Weight Loss Diet is an independent, free resource for home cooks and readers who want practical, reliable food information. Every guide on this site is written in original words, tested in a home kitchen where relevant, and kept free of copied text, sponsored claims, and made-up figures.

We focus on clear steps, realistic timings, and ingredients you can actually find. Where a recipe or method varies by region or household, we say so rather than pretending there is only one correct way.

This site is run as a small informational project. We do not sell food, supplements, or meal plans, and we do not collect personal data through these pages.

Questions or corrections? Email us at contact@weightlossdiet.github.io.
